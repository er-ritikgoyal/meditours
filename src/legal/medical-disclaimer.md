## We facilitate; we do not treat

AlisPro MediTours is a medical travel facilitator. We are not a hospital, clinic or healthcare provider. We do not diagnose, recommend treatment or give medical advice, and using our website or services does not create a doctor–patient relationship with us. Our coordinators, our WhatsApp service and any automated assistant do not answer medical questions; they pass them to a hospital or doctor.

## Treatment is between you and your doctors

Diagnosis, treatment plans, and decisions about whether, where and how to be treated are made by you with the treating hospital and doctors. They are responsible for the care they provide. Please also talk to your own doctor at home before you travel, including about whether you are fit to fly.

## About the information on this site

- Descriptions of treatments, procedures, recovery times and outcomes are general. Your own case may be different.
- Information about hospitals and doctors comes from the hospitals and public sources.
- Prices and savings are indicative only. The hospital gives you a written estimate after reviewing your reports.
- Patient stories describe one person's experience and do not promise the same result.
- Content may change and may not always be up to date.

## Not for emergencies

Do not use this website, our forms, phone line or WhatsApp in a medical emergency. Contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital.
