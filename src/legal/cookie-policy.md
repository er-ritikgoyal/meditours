This policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage alispromeditours.com uses, and how to manage them. Read it with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. "Session storage" is similar but is cleared when you close the tab.

## 2. Your choices

On your first visit a banner lets you accept all cookies, reject the optional ones, or choose by category. **Necessary** cookies are always on. **Analytics** and **Advertising** are separate, optional choices and are off until you agree. You can change your choice at any time with "Manage Cookie Preferences" on this page or in the footer, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

## 3. What we use

| Name | Category | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cc_consent` | Necessary | Remembers your cookie choices | 1 year |
| `site_locale` | Necessary | Remembers the language you picked | 1 year |
| `mt_attribution` | Session storage (ours) | Keeps the campaign tags of the link you arrived from (UTM tags, Google and Meta click identifiers), your first page and the referring site, so a form you send can tell us which campaign brought you | Until you close the tab |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Analytics (Google Analytics) | Distinguishes visitors and measures how the site is used. Set only if you accept Analytics | Up to 2 years (set by Google) |
| `_gcl_au`, `_gcl_aw`, `_gcl_gb` | Advertising (Google Ads) | Tells us which of our adverts led to an enquiry, and lets Google show relevant adverts. Set only if you accept Advertising | Up to 90 days (set by Google) |

`mt_attribution` never leaves your browser unless you send a form; it then goes to us with your enquiry. It is not a cookie and is not shared with Google.

## 4. Google consent mode

The Google tag loads on every page in "consent mode". Until you accept, it sets no Analytics or Advertising cookies and sends Google only limited signals without cookie identifiers. When you tap a WhatsApp or call button, or send a form, the tag tells Google Ads that a conversion happened. It never sends your name, contact details, message or medical information.

## 5. Other third-party content

- **Fonts** load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address but sets no cookies.
- **Videos** show a preview image from YouTube. The video itself loads from YouTube or Vimeo only when you press play; those services may then set their own cookies under their own policies.
- **WhatsApp** opens in WhatsApp or the WhatsApp website when you tap a WhatsApp button; Meta's policies then apply.

We do not control third-party cookies. Please see the providers' own policies for detail.
