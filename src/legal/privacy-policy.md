Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (the "DPDP Act") we are the **Data Fiduciary**, and under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) we are the **controller** of the personal data described here.

## 2. What we collect

**Through our enquiry forms:**

- your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (with country code) and country;
- the treatment or specialty you ask about, and your message;
- **medical reports or scans you choose to upload** (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB);
- on the insurance-eligibility form: the patient's name, age and gender, whether you have insurance, and, if you choose, a copy of your insurance card;
- a record of your consent: that you ticked the consent box, when, and which version of the wording you saw.

**When you call us or message us on WhatsApp:** your number and what you tell us, including any reports you send.

**If you go ahead with treatment:** passport and travel details, and those of anyone travelling with you, when a hospital needs them for a visa invitation letter, or we need them for accommodation or airport pickup. Please share a companion's details only with their agreement.

**Automatically, on the website:**

- the page you sent the form from, the first page you landed on, the website that referred you, and campaign tags in the link you clicked (UTM tags and Google or Meta click identifiers). These are kept in your browser for the visit and reach us only if you send a form;
- if you accept them, analytics and advertising cookies (see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy)).

**Health information is sensitive.** We ask for it only so that hospitals and doctors can review your case, and we treat it with extra care (sections 5 and 8).

## 3. Why we use it

| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| Reply to your enquiry and keep in touch about it, by email, phone or WhatsApp | Your consent, given on the form |
| Share your reports with partner hospitals and doctors so they can review your case and send a treatment plan and estimate | Your consent |
| Check whether your insurance may cover treatment | Your consent |
| Arrange visa invitation letters, travel, accommodation, interpreters and local support | Your consent; the services you ask us for |
| Follow up on your recovery after you return home | Your consent |
| Understand which adverts and pages bring enquiries | Your cookie consent; campaign tags you send with a form |
| Send news or offers | Only with separate consent, which you can refuse or withdraw at any time |
| Meet legal duties, keep the site secure and handle disputes | Legal duty; uses allowed by section 7 of the DPDP Act |

We do not sell your personal data. **We never send your medical information, or the treatment you asked about, to advertising platforms.**

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Partner hospitals and doctors** in India, only for your case and only as described in section 3.
- **Travel and support partners** (hotels, transport, interpreters), only the details each one needs.
- **Service providers** who work for us under contract:
  - Hostinger, which hosts this website and the mailbox that receives enquiry emails;
  - our customer-management system, run for us on Contabo servers in India (Supabase database and n8n automation). It receives your contact details, country, the pages and campaign tags, and your consent record, **but not your message, the treatment you asked about or any file you upload**;
  - Google (Google Ads, and Google Analytics if enabled), only for website measurement and only as your cookie choices allow, and Google Fonts, which delivers the site's fonts;
  - WhatsApp (Meta), when you choose to message us on WhatsApp;
  - YouTube or Vimeo, only when you press play on a video.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it, for example for visa processes.

## 5. Your medical reports

Reports you upload through a form are sent by email to our enquiry mailbox, which only authorised staff can open. We share them only with the hospitals and doctors reviewing your case.

## 6. Transfers outside your country

We are based in India and your data is processed in India, where our partner hospitals are. Some service providers (such as Google and Meta) may process data in other countries. Transfers from India follow the DPDP Act and any restrictions the Government notifies under it. For visitors in the EU and UK, see section 12.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Enquiries that do not lead to treatment: 2 years after our last contact.
- Medical reports and insurance cards: only as long as needed for your case, and no longer than 6 months after our last contact if you do not go ahead, or 1 year after your case is closed. You can ask us to delete them sooner. Hospitals keep their own medical records under their own legal duties.
- Passport and travel details: deleted 6 months after you return home.
- Invoices and records the law requires: for the period the law requires.
- Consent records: as long as we need to show that consent was given or withdrawn.

## 8. How we protect it

We use safeguards that suit the sensitivity of health data: encrypted connections, access limited to the coordinators handling your case, and access logs on our systems. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act and Rules require, and, where the GDPR applies, the relevant supervisory authority.

## 9. Children

If the patient is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must make the enquiry and give consent for them. We may ask for proof of guardianship. We do not use children's data for tracking or advertising.

## 10. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you, how we use it and who we have shared it with;
- correct, complete or update it;
- erase it, unless we must keep it by law;
- withdraw your consent at any time; this is as easy as giving it, and does not affect what we did before. If you withdraw, we stop processing and ask the hospitals and service providers we shared your data with to stop too;
- nominate another person to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to act.

To use any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer (section 11). We will reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

## 12. Visitors in the EU and UK

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you:

- **Lawful bases.** Health data: your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)). Other enquiry and travel data: steps you ask us to take before an agreement, and carrying out the services you ask for (Article 6(1)(b)). Site security and record-keeping: our legitimate interests and legal duties (Article 6(1)(f) and (c)). Analytics and advertising cookies: your consent.
- **Transfers.** India does not have an EU or UK adequacy decision. We transfer your data to India because it is needed to provide the service you ask for and with your explicit consent (Article 49(1)(a) and (b)).
- **Extra rights.** You can also ask us to restrict or object to processing and to give you your data in a portable form, and you can complain to the data protection authority in your country (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
- **No automated decisions.** We do not make decisions about you by automated means alone.
